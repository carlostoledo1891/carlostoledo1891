### Carlos Toledo

**Independent exact certification of machine-generated mathematics — exact arithmetic, no code shared with the claimant, refusal as a verdict.**

AI systems now announce records, constants and proofs faster than anyone reads them, and most checks compare decimals. I re-decide each claim from its published bytes in exact arithmetic, without running the claimant's code. Every claim comes back as one of three words: **CERTIFIED**, **REFUTED** with the object that breaks it, or **REFUSED** with the reason.

**What has come back from outside**

- **Cited by a registry.** Ten asterisks were removed from the optimization-constants registry (D. Davis, P. Ivanisvili, T. Tao) on my replays, in four merged pull requests, and one row was corrected on my check. The constant pages cite the replays as [T2026].
- **Credited by the claimant.** The Qiushi Engine's authors published the point sets behind their kissing bounds at the audit's request: *"Carlos Toledo has independently verified the complete configurations in all 19 dimensions."*
- **Re-derived by others.** Three outside replayers made seven re-derivations, and there was no disagreement on any row.
